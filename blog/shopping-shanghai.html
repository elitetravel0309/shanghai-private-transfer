Shopping in Shanghai: Complete Guide

Updated September 2026 · 9 min read

Shanghai is a shopper's paradise — from luxury malls to bustling street markets. Here's everything you need to know about shopping in Shanghai, from our local team at Shanghai Private Transfers.

🛍️ Best Shopping Areas

Nanjing Road (East)

What to buy: Everything — international brands, Chinese souvenirs, snacks

Type: Pedestrian shopping street

Best for: First-time visitors, souvenir shopping

Tip: Very touristy and busy. Fun to walk around but don't expect bargain prices.

Xintiandi / Taikoo Hui

What to buy: Luxury brands, designer stores, trendy boutiques

Type: Upscale shopping district

Best for: High-end shopping, cafes, people-watching

Tianzifang

What to buy: Art, crafts, souvenirs, local designer goods

Type: Art district alleyways

Best for: Unique souvenirs, arty stuff

Yuyuan Old Street

What to buy: Chinese souvenirs, tea, silk, antiques (not real!)

Type: Traditional Chinese market street

Best for: Souvenir shopping, snacks

🎁 What to Buy in Shanghai

Tea (Longjing, Pu'er)

Chinese tea is famous worldwide. Buy from reputable shops — not street stalls. Longjing (Dragon Well) green tea from Hangzhou is the most famous.

Where: Yuyuan Old Street tea shops, or go to Hangzhou tea village on a day trip

Silk Products

Shanghai and Suzhou are famous for silk. Silk scarves, pajamas, ties — great gifts.

Where: Silk shops on Nanjing Road, or Suzhou Silk Museum

Chinese Souvenirs

Chinese calligraphy, chopsticks, paper cuts, lanterns — classic gifts to bring home.

Where: Yuyuan Old Street, Tianzifang

Electronics & Gadgets

Great prices on phones, accessories, and electronics.

Where: Xujiahui electronics market

💰 Haggling Tips

  • 💴 Haggle at markets — street markets expect you to bargain. Start at 50% of the asking price.
  • 🏬 Don't haggle in malls — fixed price everywhere else.
  • 😊 Be friendly — haggling is a game, not a fight. Smile and have fun with it.
  • 🚶 Walk away — if the price isn't right, pretend to leave. Often they'll call you back with a better price.
  • 👥 Buy more = better price — buy 2-3 items and you'll get a discount.

💳 Payment Tips

  • 📱 WeChat Pay / Alipay — almost everywhere accepts these. Link your foreign card.
  • 💵 Cash still useful — for street markets and small stalls.
  • 💳 Credit cards accepted — in malls and hotels. Smaller places might not take them.
