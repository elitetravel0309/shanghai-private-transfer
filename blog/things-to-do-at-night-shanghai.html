Best Things to Do at Night in Shanghai

Updated September 2026 · 9 min read

Shanghai comes alive at night! From the iconic Bund skyline to lively night markets and rooftop bars, here are the best evening activities in Shanghai, recommended by our local team at Shanghai Private Transfers.

🌆 Top 10 Night Activities in Shanghai

1. The Bund (Waitan) Night View

Time needed: 30-60 min · Cost: Free

The #1 thing to do in Shanghai at night. The Pudong skyline lights up at 7pm — it's breathtaking. Best spot: along the Bund promenade.

Tip: Go around 7:30pm when the lights are on but it's not too late.

2. Huangpu River Cruise

Time needed: 1 hour · Cost: ¥120-200

See both sides of the river from the water. Perfect for a romantic evening or family outing.

Tip: Book the 8pm cruise for the best light show.

3. Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street

Time needed: 1-2 hours · Cost: Free

Bright neon lights, busy crowds, and endless shopping. Fun to wander around at night.

4. Rooftop Bar (The Bund / French Concession)

Time needed: 1-2 hours · Cost: ¥150-300 per person

Great views + cocktails. Popular spots: The Capitol, Bar Rouge, Rocket Bar. Perfect for date night or after-work drinks.

5. Shanghai Disneyland (Evening)

Time needed: 4-6 hours · Cost: ¥439+

Disney at night is magical — fireworks at 8:30pm! Perfect for families.

6. Acrobatic Show

Time needed: 1.5 hours · Cost: ¥180-380

Traditional Chinese acrobatics — juggling, contortion, bike stunts. Very impressive! Shows at Shanghai Circus World.

7. French Concession Evening Stroll

Time needed: 1-2 hours · Cost: Free

Tree-lined streets lit up warmly. Great cafes, bars, and boutique shops. Anfu Road and Wukang Road are the best.

8. Night Food Market

Time needed: 1-2 hours · Cost: ¥50-100 per person

Street food under the lights. Try stinky tofu, grilled skewers, and local snacks. Yuyuan Garden area has a great night market.

Safety Tips for Nighttime in Shanghai

  • 🛡️ Shanghai is very safe — one of the safest major cities in the world. You can walk around at night without worry.
  • 🚇 Metro runs until 10-11pm — after that, use taxi or DiDi.
  • 💴 Carry some cash — street food vendors and small shops don't take cards.
  • 📱 Keep phone charged — you'll use it for maps and DiDi.
