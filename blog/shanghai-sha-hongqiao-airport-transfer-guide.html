Shanghai SHA Hongqiao Airport Transfer: Complete Guide 2026

September 7, 2026 10 min read By Shanghai Private Transfer

Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA) is Shanghai's closer-to-city airport, located just 13 km west of the city center. It primarily handles domestic flights within China, as well as some regional international flights to Japan and South Korea. If you're flying domestically within China or arriving from nearby Asian destinations, chances are you'll land at Hongqiao.

In this complete guide, we compare every SHA Hongqiao airport transfer option — from private cars and taxis to the metro and airport buses — with up-to-date 2026 prices, travel times, and honest pros and cons.

Quick Answer: Best SHA Hongqiao Airport Transfer for Most Travelers

For most travelers arriving at SHA Hongqiao, a pre-booked private car transfer is the best option. Here's why:

  • Door-to-door service — your driver meets you at arrivals with a name sign
  • English-speaking driver — no language barriers
  • Flight tracking — if your flight is delayed, your driver adjusts automatically
  • Fixed price — no haggling, no surprise fees
  • Luggage assistance — your driver handles your bags
  • Convenient location — Hongqiao is much closer to the city than Pudong

SHA Hongqiao private transfer prices start from ¥300 (approximately $44 USD) for a sedan to most downtown Shanghai hotels.

Option 2: Taxi

How it works

After clearing customs and collecting luggage, follow signs to the official taxi stand. Taxis are metered, and you pay the displayed fare.

Prices (2026)

  • Daytime (5:00 AM – 11:00 PM): ¥70–¥150
  • Nighttime (11:00 PM – 5:00 AM): ¥90–¥180
  • SHA to The Bund / People's Square: ¥80–¥130
  • SHA to Lujiazui (Pudong): ¥100–¥160
  • SHA to PVG Pudong Airport: ¥180–¥250

Pros

  • Available 24/7 without booking
  • Cheaper than private transfer
  • Direct door-to-door

Cons

  • Long queues during peak hours and holidays

Important Warning

Ignore anyone inside the terminal offering "taxi" or "private car" services. These are often unlicensed operators who charge 2–3x the official rate. Always use the official taxi stand outside.

Option 3: Metro (Subway)

How it works

Hongqiao Airport is served by three metro lines: - Line 2 — connects to the city center (People's Square, Nanjing Road, Lujiazui) and continues to Pudong Airport - Line 10 — connects to the city center (Xintiandi, Yuyuan Garden, Nanjing Road) - Line 17 — connects to the western suburbs and Qingpu

The metro station is directly connected to both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 via underground walkways.

Prices (2026)

  • Single ride: ¥4–¥7 depending on distance
  • Shanghai Public Transport Card: available for purchase at metro stations (¥20 deposit + load amount)
  • Mobile payment: Alipay and WeChat Pay accepted via QR code

Schedule

  • First train: approximately 5:30–6:00 AM
  • Last train: approximately 10:30–11:00 PM
  • Frequency: every 3–8 minutes during peak hours, every 5–10 minutes off-peak

Pros

Cons

Tips for a Smooth SHA Hongqiao Arrival

  1. Know which terminal you arrive at — Hongqiao has Terminal 1 (older, mostly domestic) and Terminal 2 (newer, most flights). The two terminals are connected by a free shuttle bus or metro (one stop on Line 10).
  2. Book your transfer before you land — pre-booking ensures your driver is waiting, even if your flight is delayed.
  3. Provide your flight number — when booking, always provide your flight number. This allows the transfer company to track your flight and adjust the pickup time for delays.
  4. Have your hotel address in Chinese — even with an English-speaking driver, having your hotel's name and address in Chinese (screenshot or printed) can be helpful.
  5. Carry some cash for incidentals — while private transfers are pre-paid or paid by card, it's always good to carry some Chinese Yuan (¥) for snacks, tips, or unexpected expenses.
  6. Allow enough time for train connections — if you're connecting to a high-speed train at Hongqiao Railway Station, allow at least 2 hours between flight arrival and train departure. The station is directly connected to Terminal 2 via a 10-minute walk.
  7. Get a local SIM card or eSIM — having internet access at the airport lets you contact your driver, check your hotel, and navigate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How far is Hongqiao Airport from central Shanghai?

A: Hongqiao Airport is approximately 13–18 km from central Shanghai (The Bund / People's Square area). Travel time is 20–35 minutes by car depending on traffic. It's much closer than Pudong Airport (45 km).

Q: Which Shanghai airport should I fly into?

A: It depends on your flight. Most international flights land at Pudong (PVG), while domestic flights and some regional international flights (to Japan, South Korea) land at Hongqiao (SHA). Hongqiao is more convenient due to its proximity to the city center and direct connection to the high-speed railway station.

Q: Can I transfer directly from Hongqiao Airport to a high-speed train?

A: Yes! Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2 is directly connected to Hongqiao Railway Station via a 10-minute indoor walk. This makes it very convenient for travelers connecting to high-speed trains to Suzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Beijing, and other cities. Allow at least 2 hours between flight arrival and train departure.

Q: How do I get from Hongqiao to Pudong Airport?

A: The most convenient way is by private car or taxi (50–70 minutes, ¥180–¥250 by taxi, ¥550+ by private car). There's also an airport bus (¥30, 1.5–2 hours) and metro Line 2 (¥7, 1.5–2 hours, with a transfer at Guanglan Road for the extension to Pudong).

Q: Is Hongqiao Airport open 24 hours?

A: The airport terminals are open during flight operations, but most shops and restaurants close between 10 PM and 6 AM. The metro stops running around 11 PM, so if you arrive late, you'll need to take a taxi or pre-booked private transfer.

Q: Can I pay for a taxi with a credit card?

A: Most Shanghai taxis accept Alipay and WeChat Pay, but credit cards are generally not accepted. Carry cash or set up a mobile payment app before arrival.

Q: Do private transfers include tolls?

A: Reputable private transfer companies include all tolls, parking fees, and airport pickup fees in the quoted price. Always confirm that the price is all-inclusive before booking. For Hongqiao transfers, there are usually no tolls for city destinations.

Q: What if my flight is delayed?

A: When you provide your flight number, the transfer company monitors arrival times and adjusts pickup automatically. Most reputable companies include 60 minutes of free waiting time after landing.
