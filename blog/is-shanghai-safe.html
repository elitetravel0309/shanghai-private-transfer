Is Shanghai Safe for Travelers? Complete Safety Guide

Updated September 2026 · 8 min read

One of the most common questions from first-time visitors: "Is Shanghai safe?" The short answer: Yes, extremely safe — but there are a few things to watch out for. Here's our honest safety guide from local experts at Shanghai Private Transfers.

✅ Overall Safety Rating: 9.5/10

Shanghai is consistently ranked as one of the safest major cities in the world. Violent crime is extremely rare. You can walk around at night without worrying.

🛡️ What's Safe in Shanghai

✅ Walking around at night

Completely safe. Even late at night, the streets are well-lit and busy. You'll see families, couples, and people walking around at all hours.

✅ Pickpocketing risk

Very low. Metro stations and crowded tourist spots are the only places to be slightly careful. Keep your bag zipped and you'll be fine.

✅ Scams against tourists

Rare. The classic "tea house scam" and "art student scam" still exist but are much less common than 10 years ago. Just use common sense.

✅ Transport safety

Metro and taxis are very safe. Private car services like ours are the safest option — you know exactly who's picking you up.

⚠️ Things to Watch Out For

⚠️ Common Tourist Scams (Rare but exist)

  • "Tea house scam": Someone invites you to a "traditional tea ceremony" and the bill is $100+. Avoid anyone who approaches you on the street.
  • "Art student scam": "I'm an art student, please come see my exhibition." Then they pressure you to buy overpriced art.
  • "Fake taxi": At the airport, only use official taxi stands or pre-booked private transfers.

🚕 Taxi safety tips

  • Only use official taxis (white/blue with a roof light) or DiDi
  • Make sure the meter is running
  • Keep your belongings with you, not in the trunk
  • For peace of mind, book a private car service — no surprises

💳 Money safety

  • Use ATMs inside banks, not on the street
  • Carry only the cash you need for the day
  • WeChat Pay / Alipay are very safe — just link your card

🏥 Health & Medical Safety

  • 💧 Don't drink tap water — bottled water is everywhere, cheap and safe
  • 🏥 Hospitals are good — international hospitals in Shanghai have English-speaking doctors
  • 💊 Pharmacies everywhere — basic medicine is easy to find
  • 🦠 Air quality — usually good, check the AQI app if you're sensitive

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Safety

Shanghai is incredibly family-friendly. Kids are adored by locals — people will smile at your kids, help you with strollers, and generally be very kind. You can let your kids run around in parks without worry.
