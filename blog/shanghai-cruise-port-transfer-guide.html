Shanghai Cruise Port Transfer Guide 2026

September 7, 2026 10 min read By Shanghai Private Transfer

Shanghai is one of Asia's busiest cruise homeports, with thousands of cruise passengers embarking and disembarking every week. The main cruise terminal — Shanghai Wusongkou International Cruise Terminal — is located in Baoshan District, about 30 km north of the city center and 40 km from Pudong International Airport. Getting to and from the cruise terminal smoothly is essential to starting or ending your cruise vacation stress-free.

In this complete guide, we cover everything cruise passengers need to know about Shanghai cruise port transportation: terminal information, transfer options from airports and city hotels, prices, travel times, luggage handling, and insider tips for a smooth embarkation and disembarkation.

Option 2: Taxi / Didi

How it works

You can take a regular taxi or use the Didi app from your hotel or airport to Wusongkou Cruise Terminal. Taxis are metered, while Didi offers fixed-price estimates.

Prices (2026)

  • From The Bund / People's Square: ¥120–¥160
  • From Lujiazui: ¥130–¥170
  • From PVG Airport: ¥200–¥250
  • From SHA Airport: ¥180–¥220

Pros

  • Available without booking
  • Direct door-to-door
  • Cheaper than private transfer

Cons

  • Limited luggage space — standard taxi trunks fit 2–3 medium suitcases max; cruise passengers with 4+ bags may need 2 taxis
  • Language barrier — most taxi drivers speak little to no English; communicating the cruise terminal address can be difficult
  • Surge pricing — Didi prices can increase during peak times (cruise embarkation/disembarkation days)
  • Long wait times — on disembarkation days, taxi lines at the terminal can be 30–60 minutes
  • No flight tracking — for airport pickups

Option 3: Cruise Line Shuttle Bus

How it works

Many cruise lines offer optional shuttle bus transfers between Shanghai airports/hotels and the Wusongkou Cruise Terminal. These must be booked in advance through the cruise line or your travel agent. Buses run on fixed schedules and drop passengers directly at the terminal.

Prices (2026)

  • SHA Airport ↔ Cruise Terminal: ¥80–¥120 per person
  • City hotels ↔ Cruise Terminal: ¥50–¥100 per person (selected hotels only)

Embarkation & Disembarkation Tips

Embarkation Day (Boarding the Ship)

  1. Arrive at the terminal between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM — most cruise lines open check-in around 12:00 PM and close around 3:00 PM. Arriving early means shorter lines and more time to explore the ship before departure.
  2. Have your documents ready — passport, cruise ticket/boarding pass, visa (if required), and any health documents. Keep them in a waterproof folder for easy access.
  3. Book your transfer for 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM pickup — this gives you plenty of time to reach the terminal, check in, and board without rushing. For airport pickups, allow 1–1.5 hours after flight landing for customs and baggage claim.
  4. Label your luggage — attach the cruise line's luggage tags (provided in your pre-cruise documents) to each suitcase before arriving at the terminal. Porters will collect your luggage and deliver it to your stateroom.
  5. Carry essentials in a carry-on — medications, travel documents, electronics, a change of clothes, and any valuables should be in your carry-on, as checked luggage may not arrive at your stateroom until later in the day.

Disembarkation Day (Leaving the Ship)

  1. Choose your disembarkation time — most cruise lines assign disembarkation times based on deck level or luggage tag color. If you have an early flight, request an early disembarkation time in advance.
  2. Pack the night before — pack your checked luggage the night before disembarkation and place it outside your stateroom by the specified time (usually before midnight). Your luggage will be collected and waiting for you in the terminal.
  3. Book your pickup for 9:00 AM – 10:00 AM — most passengers disembark between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM. Your driver can wait at the terminal exit while you clear customs and collect your luggage.
  4. Allow 2–3 hours for airport flights — from disembarkation to airport check-in, allow at least 2–3 hours for domestic flights and 3–4 hours for international flights. The drive from Wusongkou to PVG takes 50–70 minutes, and to SHA takes 45–60 minutes.
  5. Consider a post-cruise city tour — if you have a late flight, consider booking a half-day city tour (The Bund, Yu Garden, Nanjing Road) with luggage storage in the vehicle before heading to the airport.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How far is Wusongkou Cruise Terminal from Shanghai city center?

A: Wusongkou International Cruise Terminal is located in Baoshan District, approximately 30 km north of Shanghai's city center (The Bund / People's Square area). Travel time is 40–55 minutes by car depending on traffic, and 90–120 minutes by public transit.

Q: How far is the cruise terminal from PVG Pudong Airport?

A: Wusongkou Cruise Terminal is approximately 45 km from PVG Pudong International Airport. Travel time is 50–70 minutes by car. If you're flying into Shanghai for a cruise, we recommend arriving at least one day before embarkation to avoid any flight delay issues. If you must arrive on embarkation day, book a flight that lands by 10:00 AM at the latest.

Q: Can I store luggage at the cruise terminal?

A: Yes, Wusongkou Cruise Terminal offers luggage storage for passengers. However, if you have a private transfer, you can leave your luggage in the vehicle while you explore the city before embarkation or after disembarkation — this is more convenient and secure than terminal storage.

Q: What time should I arrive at the cruise terminal for embarkation?

A: Most cruise lines open check-in around 12:00 PM and close around 3:00 PM. We recommend arriving between 12:00 PM and 1:30 PM for the shortest lines. Arriving too early (before 11:00 AM) may mean waiting outside, while arriving after 2:30 PM can be risky if there are delays.

Q: What time do passengers disembark on the last day?

A: Disembarkation typically starts around 7:00 AM and is completed by 10:00–10:30 AM. The exact time depends on your cruise line, deck level, and assigned disembarkation group. If you have an early flight, request an early disembarkation time (self-assist / express disembarkation) in advance, which allows you to leave the ship as early as 7:00 AM with your own luggage.

Q: Is there a shuttle bus from the airport to the cruise terminal?

A: Some cruise lines offer optional shuttle buses from PVG and SHA airports to the terminal, but these must be booked in advance through the cruise line and run on fixed schedules. There is no regular public airport shuttle bus directly to Wusongkou Cruise Terminal. A private transfer is the most reliable and convenient option.

Q: Can I take the metro to the cruise terminal?

A: The nearest metro station is Baoyang Road Station (宝杨路站) on Line 3, which is about 3 km from the cruise terminal. From there, you'll need to take a taxi or bus for the final 10-minute ride. However, we strongly do not recommend the metro for cruise passengers with luggage — hauling 2–4 large suitcases on the metro, especially during rush hour, is very difficult and exhausting. A private transfer is far more practical.

Q: How much should I tip my cruise transfer driver?

A: Tipping is not customary in China, but a small tip (¥50–¥100) for exceptional service — especially if your driver helps with multiple heavy suitcases or handles a delayed flight — is appreciated. Most private transfer prices include all fees, so tipping is optional.

Q: Can I book a transfer that includes a city tour before or after my cruise?

A: Yes! Many cruise passengers add a half-day or full-day city tour before embarkation or after disembarkation. Popular options include: The Bund, Yu Garden, Nanjing Road, Shanghai Museum, Lujiazui skyline, and Xintiandi. Your driver can store your luggage in the vehicle while you tour, then take you to the terminal or airport afterward.
