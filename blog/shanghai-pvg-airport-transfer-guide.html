Shanghai PVG Airport Transfer: The Complete Guide 2026

September 7, 2026 12 min read By Shanghai Private Transfer

Arriving at Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG) after a long international flight can be overwhelming. With two massive terminals, language barriers, and a confusing array of transportation options, many travelers wonder: What is the best way to get from PVG to my hotel in Shanghai?

In this complete guide, we compare every PVG airport transfer option — from private cars and taxis to the Maglev train, metro, and airport buses — with up-to-date 2026 prices, travel times, and honest pros and cons.

Quick Answer: Best PVG Airport Transfer for Most Travelers

For most international travelers arriving at PVG, a pre-booked private car transfer is the best option. Here's why:

  • Door-to-door service — your driver meets you at arrivals with a name sign
  • English-speaking driver — no language barriers
  • Flight tracking — if your flight is delayed, your driver adjusts automatically
  • Fixed price — no haggling, no surprise fees
  • Luggage assistance — your driver handles your bags
  • Comfortable vehicle — clean, air-conditioned sedan or MPV

PVG private transfer prices start from ¥440 (approximately $65 USD) for a sedan to most downtown Shanghai hotels.

Option 2: Taxi

How it works

After clearing customs and collecting luggage, follow signs to the official taxi stand. Taxis are metered, and you pay the displayed fare plus tolls.

Prices (2026)

  • Daytime (5:00 AM – 11:00 PM): ¥160–¥220 + tolls (~¥10–¥20)
  • Nighttime (11:00 PM – 5:00 AM): ¥200–¥280 + tolls
  • PVG to Pudong hotels: ¥120–¥180
  • PVG to The Bund / People's Square: ¥180–¥250
  • PVG to Hongqiao area: ¥250–¥350

Pros

  • Available 24/7 without booking
  • Cheaper than private transfer
  • Direct door-to-door

Cons

  • Language barrier — most taxi drivers speak little to no English
  • Long queues during peak hours (30–60 min wait)
  • Some drivers may refuse long trips or demand flat rates
  • No flight tracking — if your flight is delayed, you pay waiting time
  • Limited luggage space in standard sedans
  • Tolls paid separately in cash
Important Warning

Ignore anyone inside the terminal offering "taxi" or "private car" services. These are often unlicensed operators who charge 2–3x the official rate. Always use the official taxi stand outside.

Option 3: Maglev Train + Metro

How it works

The Shanghai Maglev (magnetic levitation) train connects PVG Airport to Longyang Road Metro Station in just 8 minutes. From Longyang Road, transfer to Metro Line 2 or Line 7 to reach your destination.

Prices (2026)

  • Maglev one-way: ¥50 (standard) / ¥100 (VIP)
  • Maglev + Metro combo ticket: ¥60–¥80
  • Single metro ride: ¥3–¥7 depending on distance

Schedule

  • First train: 6:45 AM
  • Last train: 10:30 PM
  • Frequency: Every 15–20 minutes

Pros

  • Fastest option to reach the city (8 min to Longyang Road)
  • Unique experience — world's first commercial Maglev train
  • Affordable

Cons

  • Only goes to Longyang Road — you still need metro/taxi to your hotel
  • No luggage racks — difficult with large suitcases
  • Crowded during rush hour
  • Not 24/7 — last train at 10:30 PM
  • Multiple transfers needed for most hotels

Option 4: Metro (Line 2)

How it works

Metro Line 2 runs directly from PVG Airport through central Shanghai to Hongqiao Airport and Hongqiao Railway Station. It's the cheapest option but also the slowest.

Prices (2026)

  • PVG to central Shanghai: ¥8–¥10
  • PVG to Hongqiao Airport: ¥10

Schedule

  • First train from PVG: 6:00 AM
  • Last train from PVG: 10:00 PM (approx.)

Pros

  • Cheapest option
  • Extensive network covering most of Shanghai
  • No traffic jams

Cons

  • Very slow — 90–120 minutes to central Shanghai
  • No luggage space — extremely difficult with large suitcases
  • Crowded during rush hour
  • Not 24/7
  • Multiple transfers for many hotels
  • Not recommended for travelers with heavy luggage or after long flights

Option 5: Airport Bus

How it works

Airport buses run from PVG to various locations in Shanghai, including major hotels, train stations, and bus terminals. Tickets are purchased at the airport bus counter.

Tips for a Smooth PVG Arrival

1. Book your transfer before you land

Pre-booking ensures your driver is waiting, even if your flight is delayed. Walk-up taxis can have long queues and language issues.

2. Provide your flight number

When booking, always provide your flight number. This allows the transfer company to track your flight and adjust the pickup time for delays — at no extra charge.

3. Know which terminal you arrive at

PVG has two terminals: Terminal 1 (domestic + some international) and Terminal 2 (most international). Your driver will meet you at the correct terminal's arrivals hall.

4. Have your hotel address in Chinese

Even with an English-speaking driver, having your hotel's name and address in Chinese (screenshot or printed) can be helpful for navigation.

5. Carry some cash for incidentals

While private transfers are pre-paid or paid by card, it's always good to carry some Chinese Yuan (¥) for snacks, tips, or unexpected expenses.

6. Get a local SIM card or eSIM

Having internet access at the airport lets you contact your driver, check your hotel, and navigate. China Unicom and China Telecom offer tourist SIM cards at PVG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How far is PVG from central Shanghai?

A: PVG Airport is approximately 40–50 km from central Shanghai (The Bund / People's Square area). Travel time is 45–70 minutes by car depending on traffic.

Q: Is there a high-speed train from PVG to the city?

A: The Shanghai Maglev train connects PVG to Longyang Road Station in 8 minutes, but it does not go directly to most hotels. You'll need to transfer to the metro or take a taxi from Longyang Road.

Q: Can I pay for a taxi with a credit card?

A: Most Shanghai taxis accept Alipay and WeChat Pay, but credit cards are generally not accepted. Carry cash or set up a mobile payment app before arrival.

Q: Do private transfers include tolls?

A: Reputable private transfer companies include all tolls, parking fees, and airport pickup fees in the quoted price. Always confirm that the price is all-inclusive before booking.

Q: What if my flight is delayed?

A: When you provide your flight number, the transfer company monitors arrival times and adjusts pickup automatically. Most reputable companies include 60 minutes of free waiting time after landing.

Q: Can I book a PVG transfer for a late-night arrival?

A: Yes, reputable private transfer companies operate 24/7. Late-night arrivals are no problem when booked in advance. Taxis are also available 24/7 but may have longer queues at night.

Q: Is it safe to take a taxi from PVG?

A: Official taxis from the designated taxi stand are generally safe. Avoid unlicensed drivers who approach you inside the terminal. For maximum safety and convenience, especially for first-time visitors, a pre-booked private transfer is recommended.
