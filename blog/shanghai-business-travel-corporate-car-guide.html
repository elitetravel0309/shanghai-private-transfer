Corporate Car Service Options in Shanghai

1. Executive Airport Transfer

The most common corporate service — a professional chauffeur meets you at the airport arrivals hall with a name sign, assists with luggage, and drives you directly to your hotel or office in a clean executive vehicle. Flight tracking ensures your driver adjusts for early arrivals or delays.

  • Best for: Arriving and departing business travelers, VIP clients, executives
  • Pricing: From ¥280 (city hotel) / ¥440 (PVG Airport) / ¥400 (SHA Airport) for an executive sedan

2. Hourly As-Directed Chauffeur Service

A dedicated chauffeur and vehicle at your disposal for a set number of hours. Perfect for business travelers with multiple meetings throughout the day — your driver waits while you're in meetings and takes you to the next destination. No need to hail taxis or use ride-hailing apps between meetings.

  • Best for: Full days of meetings, client visits, factory tours, multiple-stop itineraries
  • Pricing: From ¥80/hour (3-hour minimum) / ¥800 for 8 hours / ¥950 for 10 hours (executive sedan)

3. Full-Day / Half-Day Charter

A dedicated vehicle and chauffeur for a full or half day, ideal for business travelers who need transportation throughout the day but want a fixed, all-inclusive price. Includes fuel, tolls, parking, and driver allowance.

  • Best for: Conference days, client entertainment, city tours between meetings, multi-day business trips
  • Pricing: Half-day (4 hours, 50 km) from ¥500 / Full-day (8 hours, 100 km) from ¥800 (executive sedan)

4. Meeting & Event Transportation

Coordinated transportation for corporate meetings, conferences, trade shows, and company events. Includes shuttle services between hotels and venues, VIP transfers for keynote speakers, and group transportation for teams. A dedicated coordinator manages the logistics so you can focus on your event.

  • Best for: Conferences, trade shows (e.g., CES Asia, ChinaJoy), company offsites, client appreciation events
  • Pricing: Customized based on number of vehicles, hours, and logistics; from ¥800/vehicle/day

5. Intercity Business Transfer

Professional transportation for business trips between Shanghai and nearby cities such as Suzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Wuxi, and Ningbo. Perfect for factory visits, supplier meetings, and regional client visits — your chauffeur handles the driving while you work or rest in the back seat.

  • Best for: Factory visits, supplier meetings, regional client visits, multi-city business trips
  • Pricing: Shanghai to Suzhou from ¥1,000 / Shanghai to Hangzhou from ¥1,400 (executive sedan, round-trip same day)

Tips for Business Travelers in Shanghai

  1. Book airport transfers in advance — especially during conference weeks and holidays, when demand is high and wait times for taxis can be long. A pre-booked corporate transfer ensures your chauffeur is waiting when you arrive.
  2. Use hourly service for meeting-heavy days — if you have 3+ meetings in different locations, an hourly as-directed chauffeur is more cost-effective and convenient than taking multiple taxis. Your driver waits while you're in meetings and is ready when you are.
  3. Allow extra travel time — Shanghai traffic can be unpredictable, especially during rush hour (7:30–9:30 AM, 5:00–7:00 PM) and on rainy days. Allow 20–30% extra time for important meetings, or schedule meetings outside rush hour when possible.
  4. Use the car as a mobile office — executive vehicles offer a quiet, private space to take calls, answer emails, or prepare for meetings. Bring a portable charger and use the travel time productively.
  5. Request child seats if needed — if traveling with family, request child or booster seats when booking. Most corporate providers offer them at no extra charge with advance notice.
  6. Carry business cards — in Chinese business culture, exchanging business cards (mingpian) is an important ritual. Bring plenty of cards, and present and receive them with both hands as a sign of respect.
  7. Download essential apps — WeChat (for communication and payments), Didi (ride-hailing backup), Alipay (mobile payments), and a VPN (for accessing Google, Gmail, and other blocked services). Download and set up these apps before you arrive.
  8. Set up a corporate account — if your company sends employees to Shanghai regularly, a corporate account saves time, simplifies expense reporting, and ensures consistent, reliable service for all travelers.
  9. Tip your chauffeur (optional but appreciated) — while tipping is not customary in China, a small tip (¥50–¥100) for exceptional service is appreciated, especially for full-day or multi-day bookings. Most corporate clients include tips in their monthly billing or give cash directly.
  10. Know your visa requirements — most business travelers need a valid Chinese visa (M visa for business, F visa for exchanges/visits). Check the latest visa requirements and apply well in advance. Some nationalities may qualify for visa-free transit (144-hour visa-free policy for Shanghai and surrounding areas).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I get an invoice (fapiao) for corporate billing?

A: Yes, reputable corporate car service providers in Shanghai can issue official Chinese invoices (fapiao) for tax deduction purposes. Provide your company's tax information (company name, tax ID, address, phone, bank details) when setting up your account, and invoices will be issued monthly or per trip as requested.

Q: How far in advance should I book a corporate car?

A: For airport transfers, we recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance, though same-day bookings are often possible. For hourly or full-day service, book at least 48 hours in advance to ensure vehicle availability. During major conference weeks (CIIE in November, Auto Shanghai, etc.) and holidays, book 1–2 weeks in advance. Corporate account clients receive priority booking even with short notice.

Q: What is the cancellation policy for corporate bookings?

A: Most corporate car services offer free cancellation up to 2–4 hours before the scheduled pickup time. Cancellations made within 2 hours may incur a 50% charge, and no-shows are typically charged 100%. For airport pickups, the cancellation window is often longer (up to 6 hours before flight arrival) to account for flight changes. Corporate account clients may receive more flexible cancellation terms.

Q: Are the chauffeurs English-speaking?

A: Reputable corporate car services employ English-speaking chauffeurs, especially for executive and VIP service. However, English proficiency can vary. For important meetings or VIP clients, request an English-speaking chauffeur when booking, and consider adding a professional interpreter if needed for meetings (some providers offer interpreter services at an additional cost).

Q: Can I book a car for multiple days or an extended trip?

A: Yes, multi-day and weekly bookings are common for business travelers. Multi-day bookings often receive a discount (typically 10–15% off the daily rate). For extended stays (1+ week), some providers offer a dedicated chauffeur and vehicle for the duration, with customized scheduling. Contact the provider for a customized quote for multi-day or extended trips.

Q: What if my flight is delayed or my meeting runs late?

A: For airport pickups, professional services track your flight in real-time and adjust the pickup time automatically at no extra charge. For hourly service, if your meeting runs late, simply inform your chauffeur or the service provider, and the overtime will be charged pro-rata (typically per 15-minute increments). Most services offer a grace period (15–30 minutes) before overtime charges apply.

Q: Can I use a corporate car for personal use during my business trip?

A: Yes, many business travelers use their corporate car for personal activities in the evening or on weekends — dinner, sightseeing, shopping, etc. The same hourly or daily rates apply, and the charges can be billed to the company (if permitted by your company's travel policy) or paid personally. Just inform your chauffeur or the booking coordinator of your plans.

Q: How do I pay for corporate car service?

A: Payment options vary by provider but typically include: - Corporate account / monthly billing — most common for regular corporate clients; invoiced monthly with payment due in 15–30 days - Credit card — Visa, Mastercard, American Express accepted by most providers - Mobile payment — Alipay and WeChat Pay widely accepted - Cash — accepted but less common for corporate bookings - Bank transfer — for corporate accounts and large invoices
