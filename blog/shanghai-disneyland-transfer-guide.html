Shanghai Disneyland Transfer: Complete Guide 2026

September 7, 2026 11 min read By Shanghai Private Transfer

Shanghai Disneyland, located in the Pudong New Area about 30 km from the city center, is one of Asia's most popular theme parks. Whether you're flying into Shanghai for a Disney vacation or staying at a city hotel and planning a day trip, getting to the park smoothly and comfortably is essential to starting your magical day right.

In this complete guide, we compare every Shanghai Disneyland transfer option — from private cars and taxis to the metro and airport buses — with up-to-date 2026 prices, travel times, best pickup spots, and honest pros and cons.

Quick Answer: Best Shanghai Disneyland Transfer for Most Travelers

For most families and groups visiting Shanghai Disneyland, a pre-booked private car transfer is the best option. Here's why:

  • Door-to-door service — picked up from your airport or hotel, dropped directly at the park entrance
  • Family-friendly — child seats available, plenty of space for strollers and luggage
  • Early arrival — arrive before the park opens to maximize your day
  • English-speaking driver — no language barriers
  • Fixed price — no haggling, no surge pricing
  • Luggage storage — if you're arriving from the airport, leave your bags in the car

Shanghai Disneyland private transfer prices start from ¥280 from most city hotels, and from ¥450 from PVG Pudong Airport.

Option 2: Taxi / Didi

How it works

You can take a regular taxi or use the Didi app (China's equivalent of Uber) from your hotel or airport to Shanghai Disneyland. Taxis are metered, while Didi offers fixed-price estimates.

Prices (2026)

  • From The Bund / People's Square: ¥120–¥180
  • From Lujiazui: ¥100–¥150
  • From PVG Airport: ¥180–¥250
  • From SHA Airport: ¥180–¥230
  • Didi (ride-hailing): typically 10–20% cheaper than taxi

Pros

  • Available without booking
  • Direct door-to-door
  • Cheaper than private transfer

Cons

  • Language barrier — most taxi drivers speak little to no English
  • Surge pricing — Didi prices can increase during peak times (park opening/closing)
  • Long wait times — after the fireworks show, taxi lines can be 30–60 minutes
  • Limited child seats — taxis generally don't have child seats
  • Stroller hassle — folding strollers for taxi trunks can be difficult
  • No flight tracking — for airport pickups

Option 3: Metro (Line 11)

How it works

Shanghai Metro Line 11 directly serves Shanghai Disneyland. The Disney Resort Station (迪士尼站) is the terminal station of Line 11 and is located right at the park entrance. From the station, it's a 5–10 minute walk to the park gates.

Prices (2026)

  • Single ride: ¥6–¥8 depending on starting station
  • From People's Square: ¥7 (transfer to Line 11 at Jiangsu Road or other stations)
  • From Lujiazui: ¥7 (transfer to Line 11 at Century Avenue)
  • From PVG Airport: ¥8 (take Line 2 to Century Avenue, transfer to Line 11)

Schedule

  • First train to Disney: approximately 5:30–6:00 AM
  • Last train from Disney: approximately 10:00–10:30 PM (varies by day)
  • Frequency: every 5–10 minutes

Pros

  • Cheapest option
  • Direct station at park entrance
  • No traffic jams
  • Reliable schedule

Cons

  • Long travel time — 60–90 minutes from most locations
  • Transfers required from most parts of the city
  • Crowded — especially on weekends and holidays
  • No luggage space — difficult with large suitcases or strollers
  • Not 24/7 — last train around 10:30 PM, may miss the end of fireworks
  • Exhausting after a full day at the park

Option 4: Disney Resort Express Bus

How it works

Shanghai Disneyland operates express shuttle buses from several major hotels and transportation hubs in the city. These buses run on fixed schedules and drop passengers directly at the park entrance.

Prices (2026)

  • One-way: ¥30–¥50 per person
  • Round-trip: ¥50–¥80 per person
  • Children under 1.0m: free (must sit on adult's lap)

Pros

  • Affordable
  • Direct to park entrance
  • No transfers
  • More comfortable than metro

Cons

  • Fixed schedule — limited departure times
  • Limited routes — only serves specific hotels and locations
  • Must book in advance
  • Crowded — buses can fill up quickly
  • No flexibility — can't arrive early or stay late beyond schedule

Tips for a Great Shanghai Disneyland Transfer

  1. Book a round-trip private transfer — this ensures you have a ride waiting after the fireworks show, when taxi lines can be over an hour long.
  2. Arrive 30–45 minutes before park opening — this lets you be among the first to enter and ride the most popular attractions (Tron, Pirates of the Caribbean, Soaring Over the Horizon) before the crowds arrive.
  3. Stay for the evening fireworks — the "Illuminate" nighttime show is spectacular and starts around 8:30–9:00 PM. Book your return transfer for 9:30–10:00 PM.
  4. Request child seats in advance — if traveling with young children, request child or booster seats when booking your private transfer. Most reputable companies provide them at no extra charge.
  5. Bring a stroller — the park is large and involves a lot of walking. A private transfer makes stroller transport easy — no folding and hauling on the metro.
  6. Store luggage in the car — if you're arriving from the airport or heading to the airport after the park, a private transfer lets you leave your luggage in the vehicle while you enjoy the park.
  7. Avoid metro on weekends and holidays — Line 11 to Disney can be extremely crowded on weekends and Chinese holidays, making the journey unpleasant, especially with children and strollers.
  8. Download the Shanghai Disneyland app — the official app shows wait times, parade schedules, and interactive maps. It's essential for planning your day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How far is Shanghai Disneyland from the city center?

A: Shanghai Disneyland is located in Chuansha, Pudong New Area, approximately 25–30 km from the city center (The Bund / People's Square). Travel time is 30–50 minutes by car depending on traffic, and 60–90 minutes by metro.

Q: How far is Shanghai Disneyland from PVG Pudong Airport?

A: Shanghai Disneyland is approximately 25 km from PVG Pudong International Airport. Travel time is 30–40 minutes by car. This makes it very convenient to combine a Disney visit with arrival or departure flights.

Q: Can I store luggage at Shanghai Disneyland?

A: Yes, Shanghai Disneyland offers luggage storage at the park entrance for a fee (approximately ¥60–¥80 per piece per day). However, if you have a private transfer, you can leave your luggage in the vehicle for free, which is more convenient and secure.

Q: What time does Shanghai Disneyland open?

A: Shanghai Disneyland typically opens at 8:30 AM on weekdays and 9:00 AM on weekends and holidays. However, hours can vary by season and special events. Always check the official Shanghai Disneyland website or app for the exact hours on your visit date.

Q: What time does the fireworks show start?

A: The "Illuminate" nighttime fireworks and projection show typically starts at 8:30 PM or 9:00 PM, depending on the season and park hours. The show lasts approximately 20 minutes. Check the official app for the exact time on your visit date.

Q: Is the metro safe for families with children?

A: The Shanghai metro is generally safe and efficient, but it can be very crowded, especially on the Disney Resort line during peak times. For families with young children, strollers, or luggage, a private transfer is much more comfortable and convenient. If you do take the metro, avoid rush hour (7:30–9:30 AM, 5:00–7:00 PM).

Q: Can I book a private transfer just for the return trip?

A: Yes, you can book a one-way transfer from Shanghai Disneyland back to your hotel or airport. However, round-trip bookings usually receive a 10–15% discount and guarantee your return ride after the fireworks show, when taxis are scarce and wait times are long.

Q: Are there hotels near Shanghai Disneyland?

A: Yes, there are two official Disney hotels (Shanghai Disneyland Hotel and Toy Story Hotel) within the resort, plus many other hotels in the surrounding area. If you stay at a Disney hotel, you can use the free resort shuttle. If you stay at a city hotel, a private transfer is the most convenient option.
