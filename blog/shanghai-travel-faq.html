🛂 Visa & Entry

Do I need a visa to visit Shanghai?
Most nationalities qualify for 240-hour (10-day) visa-free transit if you're transiting through China. If Shanghai is your final destination, you'll need a tourist visa (L Visa).
What documents do I need for 240-hour visa-free transit?
Valid passport (6+ months), confirmed onward ticket leaving China within 10 days, and entry/exit through Shanghai airport.

✈️ Getting There & Around

How do I get from Pudong Airport (PVG) to downtown?
Options: Metro Line 2 (¥7, 1 hour), Maglev train (¥50, 8 min to Longyang Rd), taxi (¥150, 45 min), or private transfer (¥280+, door-to-door with English-speaking driver).
How do I get from Hongqiao Airport (SHA) to downtown?
Much closer than Pudong! Metro Line 2/10 (¥5, 30 min), taxi (¥60, 20 min), or private transfer (¥180, door-to-door).
Is it easy to get around Shanghai without speaking Chinese?
Yes! Metro signs are in English + Chinese. Taxis are hit-or-miss for English. Private car services have English-speaking drivers — the easiest option.

🏨 Accommodation

What's the best area to stay in Shanghai?
For first-time visitors: People's Square or The Bund area. Best for couples: French Concession. Best for shopping: Jing'an. Best for business: Lujiazui.

🍜 Food & Drink

What food is Shanghai famous for?
Xiaolongbao (soup dumplings), shengjian (pan-fried buns), Shanghai-style noodles, hongshao rou (braised pork belly), and scallion oil pancakes.
Do restaurants have English menus?
Tourist areas and international restaurants do. Local restaurants might not — point at what other people are eating, or use your phone to translate.

💰 Money & Costs

How much money do I need per day in Shanghai?
Budget: ¥300-500/day (hostel + street food + metro). Mid-range: ¥800-1,500/day (nice hotel + restaurants + private car occasionally). Luxury: ¥2,000+/day.
Can I use USD or Euros in Shanghai?
No, you need Chinese Yuan (RMB). Exchange money at airports, banks, or use ATMs. WeChat Pay and Alipay accept foreign cards too.

🛡️ Safety & Health

Is Shanghai safe at night?
Yes, very safe. You can walk around at any time without worrying. Shanghai is one of the safest major cities in the world.
Can I drink the tap water in Shanghai?
No, drink bottled water only. It's cheap and available everywhere.

📱 Connectivity

Can I use Google / Facebook / Instagram in Shanghai?
No, these are blocked in China. Download a VPN before you arrive, or use local alternatives (WeChat, Weibo, Baidu).
Should I get a local SIM card?
Yes, or buy an eSIM before you arrive. Data plans are cheap (¥50-100 for 10GB). You'll need data for maps, WhatsApp, and translations.
