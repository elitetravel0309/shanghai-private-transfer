Tips for a Great Shanghai Family Tour

  1. Book a full-day private car with child seats — Shanghai's best family attractions are spread across the city. A full-day car (8–10 hours) with child seats lets you explore comfortably without worrying about transportation or safety.
  2. Request child seats in advance — Always specify your children's ages and weights when booking so the appropriate car seats or boosters can be installed before pickup. Most reputable services provide them free of charge.
  3. Book an MPV or van for families with 2+ kids — Sedans can be cramped with multiple car seats, strollers, and diaper bags. An MPV (7-seater) or van offers much more space and comfort for the whole family.
  4. Plan your itinerary by age group — Choose attractions that match your children's ages and interests. Toddlers love aquariums and zoos; school-age kids love science museums and theme parks; teens enjoy shopping, skyline views, and trendy neighborhoods.
  5. Don't overschedule — Kids (and parents!) get tired. Plan 2–3 main attractions per day with plenty of breaks, meals, and downtime. It's better to fully enjoy 2 attractions than rush through 4.
  6. Bring snacks and water — Shanghai can be hot and humid, especially in summer. Pack snacks, water bottles, and wet wipes for the kids. Your driver can stop at convenience stores for refills.
  7. Take advantage of stroller-friendly attractions — Many Shanghai museums and parks are stroller-friendly. Bring a lightweight stroller for toddlers, and your driver can store it in the car when not in use.
  8. Ask your driver for family-friendly recommendations — Local drivers know the best family-friendly restaurants, rest areas, playgrounds, and hidden gems. They can also help with restaurant reservations and attraction tickets in Chinese.
  9. Visit popular attractions early or late — Shanghai Disneyland, the Science Museum, and the zoo are most crowded between 10 AM and 3 PM. Arrive at opening or visit in the late afternoon for shorter lines and a more relaxed experience.
  10. Be prepared for weather — Shanghai has four distinct seasons. Summer (June–August) is hot and humid — bring sunscreen, hats, and water. Winter (December–February) is cold — dress in layers. Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons for family travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you provide child seats and boosters?

A: Yes! We provide age-appropriate child seats and boosters free of charge for children of all ages. Simply specify your children's ages and weights when booking, and we'll install the appropriate seats before pickup. We offer infant seats (0–13 kg), convertible seats (9–18 kg), and booster seats (15–36 kg).

Q: What is the best age to bring kids to Shanghai Disneyland?

A: Shanghai Disneyland is fun for all ages, but kids ages 4–12 tend to enjoy it the most. Toddlers (under 3) get free admission and can enjoy gentle rides, character meet-and-greets, and parades, but may be too small for many rides. Teens and adults also love the thrilling rides (TRON, Pirates of the Caribbean, Soaring Over the Horizon) and the evening fireworks show.

Q: Can we bring a stroller into attractions?

A: Most Shanghai attractions are stroller-friendly, including the Shanghai Zoo, Shanghai Ocean Aquarium, Shanghai Science and Technology Museum, and Shanghai Natural History Museum. Strollers are also allowed at Shanghai Disneyland (you can rent them there if you don't want to bring your own). Some smaller museums and historic sites may have limited stroller access due to stairs and narrow passages. Your driver can help store the stroller in the car when visiting attractions that aren't stroller-friendly.

Q: Are there family-friendly restaurants in Shanghai?

A: Yes! Shanghai has a wide range of family-friendly restaurants, including: - International chains: McDonald's, KFC, Pizza Hut, Starbucks (all widely available) - Kid-friendly Chinese restaurants: Din Tai Fung (xiaolongbao), Crystal Jade (Cantonese), and many others with kids' menus - Buffet restaurants: Hotel buffets and international buffets with wide variety for picky eaters - Restaurants with play areas: Some malls and family-friendly restaurants have play areas for kids Your driver can recommend family-friendly restaurants near each attraction and help with reservations.

Q: How many days should we spend in Shanghai with kids?

A: We recommend 3–5 days for a family trip to Shanghai: - Day 1: Shanghai Disneyland (full day) - Day 2: Classic family tour (zoo, aquarium, museum, The Bund) - Day 3: Science & nature day (Science Museum, Wild Animal Park) or day trip to Suzhou/Hangzhou - Day 4 (optional): Relaxed day with shopping, parks, and cultural experiences A private car with driver makes multi-day family travel much easier and more comfortable.

Q: Is Shanghai safe for families with kids?

A: Yes, Shanghai is generally a very safe city for families with children. Crime rates are low, and the city is well-maintained and clean. However, as with any big city, take standard precautions: keep an eye on your kids in crowded areas, hold hands when crossing streets, and keep valuables secure. Your private driver can also provide safety tips and help navigate crowded areas.

Q: Can we do a day trip from Shanghai with kids?

A: Yes! There are several great family-friendly day trip destinations from Shanghai: - Suzhou (1.5 hours): Beautiful gardens, museums, and canal boat rides — great for kids who love nature and history - Hangzhou (2–2.5 hours): West Lake boat rides, tea plantations, and parks — perfect for a relaxing family day - Wuzhen (1.5–2 hours): Ancient water town with boat rides, traditional crafts, and cultural experiences A private car with driver is the most comfortable way to do day trips with kids, with child seats, flexible scheduling, and no need to navigate public transit with strollers and luggage.

Q: What should we pack for a family trip to Shanghai?

A: Essential items for a family trip to Shanghai include: - For kids: Comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, sunscreen, hats, snacks, water bottles, wet wipes, hand sanitizer, any medications, favorite small toys or books for waiting times - For babies/toddlers: Diapers, formula, baby food, bottles, pacifiers, lightweight stroller, baby carrier - For the family: Passports, travel documents, hotel confirmations, attraction tickets (if pre-booked), power bank, camera, umbrella or rain jackets - Optional: Chinese phrasebook or translation app, local SIM card or portable WiFi, small gifts for new friends Your private car provides a safe, comfortable space to store all your family gear during the day.
