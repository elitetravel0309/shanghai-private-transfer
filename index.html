      Private airport pickup

      Shanghai Airport Transfer

      Private Shanghai airport transfers with flight tracking, meet and greet, luggage planning, English-friendly support, and clear written quotes.

      Plan with confidence

      Arrive in Shanghai with a confirmed private chauffeur, clear meeting instructions, and a vehicle selected for your passengers and luggage. We coordinate arrivals and departures at both Pudong International Airport and Hongqiao International Airport, with direct service to hotels, offices, residences, cruise terminals, railway stations, and nearby cities.

      01

      A clear arrival plan before you fly

      Your confirmation includes the airport, terminal, pickup time, meeting instructions, chauffeur contact method, vehicle category, and waiting-time terms. Sharing your flight number allows our team to monitor arrival updates and coordinate the pickup more accurately.

      02

      A vehicle matched to people and luggage

      An airport transfer needs enough usable luggage space, not only enough seats. Tell us the number of adults, children, checked suitcases, cabin bags, strollers, or special equipment. We will recommend an executive sedan, luxury MPV, van, or minibus that fits the real travel party.

      03

      Direct service beyond central Shanghai

      Airport pickups can continue directly to Shanghai districts, Disney hotels, cruise terminals, exhibition venues, Suzhou, Hangzhou, Wuzhen, and other Yangtze River Delta destinations. Multi-stop plans and return transfers can be included in one written itinerary.

      Simple booking process

      Send the real journey information and we will recommend the vehicle and written service plan:

      1

      Date and pickup time

      Your scheduled arrival or departure.

      2

      Route and flight details

      Airports, terminals and flight numbers.

      3

      Passengers and luggage

      Adults, children, suitcases and special equipment.

      4

      Vehicle and special requests

      Child seats, name signs, or amenities.

      Private Disney ride

      Shanghai Disney Transfer

      Private transfers to Shanghai Disney Resort with family-friendly vehicles, child-seat requests, luggage planning, and flexible return pickup.

      Plan with confidence

      Make a Shanghai Disney day easier with private door-to-door transport. We can arrange hotel pickup, airport-to-resort transfers, family luggage space, child-seat requests, and a confirmed return pickup after the park closes or your dinner finishes.

      01

      Door-to-door travel for families

      Avoid carrying children, strollers, and bags through multiple transport connections. A private vehicle collects your party from the hotel, residence, airport, or railway station and travels directly to the confirmed Disney drop-off area.

      02

      Plan the return before entering the park

      Disney closing times, evening shows, and dining plans can affect pickup. We agree on a practical return plan before the trip and share the communication method for final coordination near the end of your visit.

      03

      Space for strollers and holiday luggage

      A vehicle that seats the family may not hold every suitcase and stroller. Share the full luggage list for airport or hotel-change transfers so we can recommend a suitable MPV, van, or minibus.

          Cancellation & Refund Policy

          We understand travel plans can change. Please notify us as early as possible so we can help adjust your arrangements or confirm any applicable refund.

          How to cancel or change a booking

          Send your request by email or WhatsApp using the contact details on this website. A cancellation or change is effective only at the time we confirm receipt in writing, not when a message is first sent. Include your name, service date, and booking reference where available.

          1

          More than 48 hours before pickup

          Eligible for a full refund of prepaid service charges.

          2

          24 to 48 hours before pickup

          Eligible for a 50% refund of prepaid service charges.

          3

          Less than 24 hours before pickup

          Prepaid service charges are non-refundable.

          4

          No-show or cancellation after dispatch

          No-show, refusal of service, or cancellation after vehicle dispatch: non-refundable.

          Bookings involving Toyota Alphard, Volvo EM90, Mercedes-Benz S-Class, peak dates, exhibitions, long-distance travel, or multi-day charters may have different cancellation terms. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible; processing may take 5 to 10 business days.
